# miniLight — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-18_

miniLight is a film‑camera light meter. It is designed to work entirely on
your device. We do not run servers and we do not collect, transmit, or sell
any personal data.

## What the app accesses

- **Camera** — used only to measure light for exposure readings. Camera
  frames are processed in real time on the device and are **not stored,
  saved, or transmitted**. No photos are recorded.
- **Ambient‑light sensor (Android)** — used only to provide incident‑style
  lux readings. Values stay on the device.
- **Location (optional)** — only if you save a shot to the shot log, the
  app may attach the current GPS coordinates to that log entry so you can
  remember where a frame was taken. Location is requested only at that
  moment, is stored **locally on your device**, and is never uploaded.

## Data storage

- Settings, custom film stocks, calibration, and the shot log (including
  any GPS coordinates you chose to attach) are stored **locally** using the
  device's standard app storage.
- CSV export is **user‑initiated**: a file is created only when you tap
  Export, and it is shared through the system share sheet to a destination
  you choose. The app does not send it anywhere on its own.

## Data sharing

We do **not** share data with third parties. The app contains **no
analytics, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs**. No data leaves your
device except files you explicitly export and share yourself.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including
children.

## Your control

You can delete all stored data at any time by deleting rolls within the app
or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page

## Contact
