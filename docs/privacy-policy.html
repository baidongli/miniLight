miniLight — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-18

miniLight is a film-camera light meter. It is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not run servers and we do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Camera — used only to measure light for exposure readings. Camera frames are processed in real time on the device and are not stored, saved, or transmitted. No photos are recorded.
  • Ambient-light sensor (Android) — used only to provide incident-style lux readings. Values stay on the device.
  • Location (optional) — only if you save a shot to the shot log, the app may attach the current GPS coordinates to that log entry. Location is requested only at that moment, stored locally on your device, and never uploaded.

Data storage

Settings, custom film stocks, calibration, and the shot log (including any GPS coordinates you chose to attach) are stored locally using the device's standard app storage. CSV export is user-initiated: a file is created only when you tap Export and is shared through the system share sheet to a destination you choose.

Data sharing

We do not share data with third parties. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs. No data leaves your device except files you explicitly export and share yourself.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Your control

You can delete all stored data at any time by deleting rolls within the app or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the app's store listing support contact.
